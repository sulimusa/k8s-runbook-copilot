# Datadog historical Kubernetes incident pattern

Original project-authored synopsis. Status: historical analogy, not current service guidance.
Pattern ID: P01

Cross-node pod connections timed out after a routine maintenance window. Historical context: Datadog reports that a systemd-networkd restart removed pod routing rules during unattended updates across environments. Useful checks: Compare host routing and maintenance history with cloud firewall decisions for the failed packet path.
