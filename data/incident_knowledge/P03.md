# Skyscanner historical Kubernetes incident pattern

Original project-authored synopsis. Status: historical analogy, not current service guidance.
Pattern ID: P03

Services vanished from the cluster shortly after an infrastructure change. Historical context: Skyscanner reports a templating change producing an empty desired state and automated reconciliation deleting resources. Useful checks: Inspect rendered desired state and the identity, timestamps and targets of resource deletion audit events.
