# Grafana Labs historical Kubernetes incident pattern

Original project-authored synopsis. Status: historical analogy, not current service guidance.
Pattern ID: P04

A rolling update stalled while new pods repeatedly ran out of memory. Historical context: Grafana reports a Service selector excluding old replicas, concentrating traffic on a small updated subset and causing OOMs. Useful checks: Compare Service selectors and EndpointSlices with per-pod traffic and equal-workload memory profiles.

Original synopsis and diagnostic suggestions; historical analogy is not proof of a current cause.
