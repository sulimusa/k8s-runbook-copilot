# GitLab historical Kubernetes incident pattern

Original project-authored synopsis. Status: historical analogy, not current service guidance.
Pattern ID: P06

A running Redis pod became unreachable through its internal load balancer. Historical context: GitLab reports that a PDB prevented Redis pod eviction while a GKE controller independently removed its load-balancer endpoint. Useful checks: Compare direct pod reachability, endpoint-group membership, target health and network-policy drop evidence.

Original synopsis and diagnostic suggestions; historical analogy is not proof of a current cause.
