# Preply historical Kubernetes incident pattern

Original project-authored synopsis. Status: historical analogy, not current service guidance.
Pattern ID: P10

Some pods have intermittent DNS timeouts after CoreDNS scaled down. Historical context: Preply reports DNS failures after CoreDNS scale-down with traffic directed to a removed endpoint. The underlying conntrack cleanup failure was not conclusively established. Useful checks: Inspect failing flow destinations and policy decisions; do not claim the underlying conntrack cleanup defect without more evidence.

Original synopsis and diagnostic suggestions; historical analogy is not proof of a current cause.
