# Applied Computing Research Labs historical Kubernetes incident pattern

Original project-authored synopsis. Status: historical analogy, not current service guidance.
Pattern ID: P11

A simulation finished its workload but never completed cleanup. Historical context: ACRL reports a fail-open webhook certificate race followed by mutation errors: ordinary Pod updates failed on nodeSelector changes and kubelet status updates failed on label changes. Useful checks: Inspect admission timing and rules, accepted status updates, deletionTimestamp, finalizers and their owning controller.
